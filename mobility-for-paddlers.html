Mobility for paddlers

Keep paddling. Hurt less doing it.

A class for mature paddlers, and for anyone who wants to keep walking, skiing, and golfing. It balances the strength work of the sport so daily life and the next paddle both feel better.

What the class does for you

I raced dragon boats and I still paddle an outrigger on Georgian Bay, so this class comes from the boat as well as from the studio. You work on the ranges and the control that paddling, walking, and other long-loved sports quietly take away: ribs, shoulders, hips, and the ability to rotate without forcing it.

It has run online since 2020. You can join from anywhere. If your crew wants the same session together, ask about a team rate.

45 to 50 minutes, twice a week, booked by the month. Single class $25 plus tax.

Monthly packages, bulk rates, and team rates for eight or more paddlers from the same team. A private in-person class is also available.

Ask about the next class

I also teach Pilates and yoga-based mobility at paddle camps. Those dates live on the training camps page. If you want one-to-one help as well as the class, start with online coaching or personal training.

Questions

Is this only for dragon boat paddlers?

It started there, and it helps anyone who wants to keep walking, skiing, golfing, or paddling. Many of the people in the class are older adults.

When and where?

The class runs twice a week on a monthly basis, for 45 to 50 minutes, on Zoom. A private in-person class can also be arranged.

What does it cost?

A single class is $25 plus tax. Monthly packages, bulk rates, and team rates for groups of eight or more from the same team are available. Longer blocks of one, two, or three months are priced by the length of the session.

Why mobility, if I already train hard?

Paddling asks a lot of the same patterns, over and over. This class balances that work so you can stay on the water with less pain and a body that still rotates, reaches, and recovers.

How do I join?

Write to me and say you want Mobility for Paddlers. If you are booking a team, say how many people and which crew.

I coach older adults in person around South Georgian Bay, and online anywhere.
