Small group training

Friends, attention, and a plan that still sees you.

A group of three or four older adults. You get the pleasure of company and more coaching than a crowded class can offer.

I move from person to person. If your knee does not like an exercise, you are not left to modify it alone. If your friend needs a different cue, they get that too. You leave having worked, and having been seen.

People choose this when they want to spend the hour with friends, keep the cost down, and still get the detailed attention that helps older adults move safely and with more confidence.

From $52 per person. Packages of 5 to 40 sessions are available.

55-minute sessions, in person or online. Rates are before tax.

Ask about a small group

Questions

How many people?

Three or four. Small enough that I can move from person to person. Large enough that it feels social.

Is this a big fitness class?

No. A larger class cannot give this kind of detail. In a group of three or four, I can still correct, encourage, and change an exercise for you.

What does it cost?

From $52 per person. Packages of 5 to 40 sessions are available. Sessions are 55 minutes unless noted, in person or online, before tax.

Who enjoys this most?

Older adults who like training with friends and still want someone watching their form. It is also a comfortable way to start if a fully private session feels like a big step.

Prefer two people only? Semi-private training starts at $57 per person. Local drop-in style classes are listed on the classes page.

I coach older adults in person around South Georgian Bay, and online anywhere.
