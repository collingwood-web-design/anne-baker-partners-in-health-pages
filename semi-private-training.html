Semi-private training

Bring one person with you.

Two older adults, one coach. You get a personal plan and a lower rate than a fully private session, which makes it easier to practice often enough to feel a change.

What you get

The session is still individualized. I am not running a class at you. I watch both of you, cue what each body needs, and give you stretches of work you can do side by side. The company helps. The lower cost helps you book the next one.

This is a good fit if a spouse, friend, or paddling partner wants the same thing you do: less pain, better balance, and strength that shows up in real life.

From $57 per person each session. Packages of 5 to 40 sessions are available.

55 minutes, in person at home or online. Rates are before tax.

Book a partner session

Questions

Who is in the session?

You and one other person. Many pairs are friends, partners, or neighbours who want to stay active together.

Is it still personal?

Yes. You share the hour, and you also do some of the work on your own while I move between you. You still get my eyes on your movement.

What does it cost?

From $57 per person for a 55-minute session, plus tax. Packages of 5 to 40 sessions are available.

Can we do this online?

Yes. Partner sessions are available in person at home around South Georgian Bay, or online.

Why choose this instead of private training?

The lower rate makes it easier to come more often, and training with someone you like often keeps you showing up. More consistent practice usually beats the occasional perfect session.

Want the hour to yourself? See personal training. Want three or four people? See small group training.
