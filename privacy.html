Privacy policy

This policy is for annebakerpartnersinhealth.com, the website of Anne Baker Partners in Health.

Who I am

I am Anne Baker. Partners in Health is my movement coaching practice for older adults, based in South Georgian Bay, Ontario, and available online. You can reach me at bakerford@sympatico.ca or 705-441-2258.

What you send me

If you use a form on this site, you choose what to send: your name, email, phone, and message. I use that to reply, and, if you asked for notes or agreed at the form, to send occasional emails about classes, camps, and coaching. You can unsubscribe or ask me to delete those details.

The form is delivered by Collingwood Web Design’s form service so the message reaches me. This page does not ask you to create an account.

Cookies and other services

This site has no member login and no comment section, so it does not set those kinds of cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from media.cwd-cdn.com. Those services may receive ordinary technical information, such as an IP address, when your browser requests the file.

How long we keep a message

I keep form messages long enough to answer you and to meet ordinary business and legal duties. I do not keep them for their own sake.

Your choices

Email bakerford@sympatico.ca if you want a copy of the contact details a form collected about you, or if you want them deleted. That does not include records we are required to keep.

Photos

Please do not send photographs that still contain location information if you would not want that information shared. Studio photographs on this site are credited to Kimberly Vincent Photography where they are hers.

I coach older adults in person around South Georgian Bay, and online anywhere.
