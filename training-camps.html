Training camps

Mobility teaching where paddlers already gather.

Camps are one of the ways people find me. I teach Pilates and yoga-based mobility to paddlers who want to stay durable in the boat and comfortable when they get out of it.

Many of the paddlers I support are older adults who still race, tour, or train with a crew. The camp work is hands-on and short: enough to change how you feel that week, and enough to give you a practice you can keep.

Where I teach

  • Space Coast Dragon Boat Camp
  • Dragon Boat East training camp

In recent years those camps have been in Florida and Nova Scotia, in April and July. I will add the next confirmed dates on this page. Register for a camp through the camp, not through this website.

Next camp dates: to be confirmed. If you are choosing a camp because I am teaching, ask me which one I am joining.

Between camps, the ongoing class is Mobility for Paddlers, online, twice a week when a month is running. One-to-one help is on the online coaching page.

Questions

What do you teach at camp?

Pilates and yoga-based mobility, so you can balance the hours you spend in the boat. This is the same idea as my Mobility for Paddlers class, taught in person with the camp.

Which camps?

I teach each year at Space Coast Dragon Boat Camp and at Dragon Boat East. In recent seasons that has meant Florida and Nova Scotia, in April and July.

Are the next dates posted?

Not yet. When I confirm the next camp dates, I will list them here. The camp websites are the place to register for the camp itself.

Can I work with you if I cannot get to camp?

Yes. Mobility for Paddlers is online all year, and personal coaching is available on Zoom.

I coach older adults in person around South Georgian Bay, and online anywhere.
