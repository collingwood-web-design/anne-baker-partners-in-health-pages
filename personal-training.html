Personal training

One-to-one coaching for your body, not a template.

A private session gives me time to see how you move and to build a plan around your joints, your energy, and the life you want to keep living.

What this does for you

You get my eyes on you. That matters when a shoulder pinches, a hip feels guarded, or balance is the thing you no longer trust. I use the session to help you move with less pain and more ease, and from that ease, more strength and stability.

Most of the people I see privately are older adults. You might want to get down to the floor and back up, walk farther, garden, travel, paddle, ski, or simply feel less brittle in the morning. Tell me that. The session starts there.

I mix personal training, Pilates, yoga, and mobility, and I will slow a movement down until you can feel it. Slow gets the brain’s attention. Better is better than more.

$65 for 30 minutes. $97 for 55 minutes. Packages of 5 to 40 sessions are available. Rates are before tax.

In your home around South Georgian Bay, or online. Sessions are 55 minutes unless you choose the shorter one.

Book personal training

A session, in plain language

You will not be handed a workout and left to figure it out. I explain what I want you to notice — a rib cage that can move, a hip that is not bracing, a foot that knows where it is. You practice. I adjust. You leave knowing what to repeat before you see me again.

If you would rather not have me in your home, book the same private coaching on Zoom. The online coaching page explains how that works.

If you want company and a lower rate, look at semi-private training for two people or a small group of three or four.

Questions older adults ask

Is personal training only for people who are already fit?

No. I start where you are. Many of the people I see are older adults returning to movement after a stiff season, an injury, or years of putting themselves last.

Where do we meet?

In your home around South Georgian Bay, or online. You do not need a gym membership for a private session with me.

How long is a session?

55 minutes, unless you book the 30-minute option. Rates are before tax.

What does it cost?

$65 for 30 minutes and $97 for 55 minutes. Packages of 5 to 40 sessions are available.

What should I expect?

I watch how you move, explain what I want you to feel, and adjust the plan. The goal is less pain, more ease, and strength you can use in daily life.

How do I book?

Send me a note on the contact page and say you want personal training. I will confirm times and whether you would rather meet at home or online.

I coach older adults in person around South Georgian Bay, and online anywhere.
