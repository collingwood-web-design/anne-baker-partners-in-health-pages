Online coaching

Coaching for older adults, wherever you are.

You should not have to live in South Georgian Bay to work with me. Virtual sessions are a real part of my practice, for people who want strength, mobility, and confidence at home.

Why older adults choose Zoom

Winter roads, a week away, a hip that does not love the car, or a town with no suitable class nearby. Online coaching removes the trip and keeps our work together. I can still see how you move, slow you down, and tell you what to feel.

I have taught Mobility for Paddlers online since 2020. The same approach — clear, unhurried, and specific — is available for personal training and small groups.

Personal training: $65 / 30 minutes or $97 / 55 minutes.

Semi-private: from $57 per person. Small group: from $52 per person. All before tax.

What a first online session is like

You tell me what you want to be able to do, and what currently gets in the way. I choose movements you can see and copy on a screen. You will hear where the effort should be, and where it should not. If something is not clear, you stop and we reset. The point is a practice you understand well enough to repeat on your own.

If you paddle, the Mobility for Paddlers class is the online group built for that. If you want a set class in Collingwood instead, see local classes.

Questions

Do I need to be in Collingwood?

No. Online coaching is for older adults anywhere. If you can join a Zoom call, you can train with me.

What can be done online?

Personal training, semi-private sessions for two, small groups of three or four, and Mobility for Paddlers. Local studio classes at Active Life Conditioning and the Leisure Time Club stay in person.

What do I need at home?

Enough floor space to lie down and stand up safely, and a way to see and hear me. A chair, a mat, and a couple of household props are often enough. I will tell you what to have nearby before you start.

Is online as personal as a home visit?

You still get my eyes on you and a plan that changes with what I see. A camera is not the same as standing beside you, and I will say so if a movement should wait until we meet in person.

What does it cost?

Personal training is $65 for 30 minutes and $97 for 55 minutes. Semi-private is from $57 per person. Small group is from $52 per person. Mobility for Paddlers single classes are $25, plus tax. Packages are available.

How do we start?

Write to me and say you want online coaching, plus whether you prefer private, partner, group, or the paddlers’ class. I will reply with times.

I coach older adults in person around South Georgian Bay, and online anywhere.
