Common questions

Who is this for?

People who want to move with less pain, keep their strength, and stay active through every stage of life. Mature paddlers are welcome too.

Do I have to live in Collingwood?

No. In-person sessions are around South Georgian Bay, including in your home. Personal training, partner sessions, small groups, and Mobility for Paddlers are also online.

How do I choose?

Start with one-to-one if you want a plan that is only yours. Choose a partner or small group if you like company and a lower rate. Choose a class if you want a set weekly time in Collingwood.

How do I book?

Use the contact form and say which option you are considering. I will reply with times and what to expect. Class bookings at Active Life Conditioning and the Leisure Time Club go through those studios.

I help people stay strong and active through every stage of life, in person around South Georgian Bay and online anywhere.

Land acknowledgement

For more than 15,000 years the First Nations walked upon, and cared for, the lands we now call home: Anishinaabek, Haudenosaunee, Ojibwe, Petun, Wendat and many others who cared for their families and communities, the way we now seek to care for ours. Partners in Health acknowledge the Lake Simcoe-Nottawasaga Treaty of 1818 and respects all the Nation-to-Nation agreements that have formed relationships with the original inhabitants of Turtle Island. We seek to do better, to continue to recognize, learn, and grow, in friendship and community, Nation-to-Nation.
